Most serious weaknesses

No independent biological unit.
All 18 RNA-seq libraries come from one experiment/cohort. Evidence needed: a genuinely independent fish cohort collected under a locked protocol.

Author-selected transcript lists are being treated as if they were discoveries.
Reproducing 1750/172/52 proves faithful extraction/recalculation of the publication, not independent discovery. Evidence needed: independently processed per-sample expression data with a prespecified analysis producing a result not simply inherited from the authors' highlighted lists.

No held-out outcome.
There is no fish-level test set linking molecular measurements to an outcome unavailable during model development. Evidence needed: frozen, previously untouched fish with both predictor and outcome measurements.

No fair comparator.
There is no actual comparison against the strongest named published growth model or a prespecified ordinary TGC/degree-day model. Evidence needed: identical fish/task/outcome, identical train/test partition, locked metrics and the published comparator implemented under equivalent information constraints.

Same-cohort thesis material cannot establish external replication.
Fillet/liver and related thesis measurements originate from the same overarching cohort. Evidence needed: independent biological replication, not merely another assay or dissertation analysis of the same animals.

The independent literature does not solve the validation problem.
The 2014 heat study, 2013 juvenile GH-triploid microarray study, and 2019 antiviral qPCR study address different exposure, developmental, tissue, challenge, or endpoint contexts. Evidence needed: a study matching the project's exact prediction/measurement task.

Mechanistic inference is underpowered by design.
Correlated temperature-associated expression and growth in the same 18-fish experiment cannot distinguish mechanism from temperature response, cohort effects, or downstream consequences. Evidence needed: independently measured intermediate variables plus a preregistered mechanistic prediction tested in new observations.

The apparent RNA-read discrepancy is not a finding.
The exact 2× relationship between paper mate-read counts and ENA fragment counts is a metadata/unit reconciliation. Evidence needed for biological novelty would be an independently derived biological signal surviving appropriate sample-level analysis.

Tool readiness is not demonstrated by an audit alone.
Source/audit tables establish provenance, not that another researcher can use the system to obtain a validated scientific prediction. Evidence needed: documented input → analysis → output workflow plus an independently reproduced result and a frozen test.

The stated project completion gates remain materially unmet.
The packet itself reports open floors for 40 genuinely applied services, 120 individually fetched/used accession payloads, 10 audited derivations, 50 substantive rendered pages, a positive fair comparator, and discovery. These are not cosmetic omissions if they are locked project gates.

One fresh prespecified published-data pivot

Temperature–growth trade-off replication/audit using only genuinely independent published datasets, without changing the original failed hypothesis.

Before looking at results, lock:

the exact endpoint: e.g. temperature-normalized growth coefficient or FCR;

eligible study criteria: Atlantic salmon, specified temperature exposure, GH-transgenic/AquAdvantage-relevant population where available;

a fixed temperature-response model;

one conventional comparator;

effect-size and heterogeneity calculations;

exclusion rules;

no post hoc selection of favorable transcripts or temperatures.

Then use newly retrieved published datasets/studies not belonging to the original 18-fish cohort to ask a narrow question: Does the reported temperature-associated growth trade-off reproduce at the level of the published growth phenotype?

Importantly, this does not retroactively rescue a failed discovery or benchmark gate. If the independent literature does not support the prespecified effect, retain the negative result.
