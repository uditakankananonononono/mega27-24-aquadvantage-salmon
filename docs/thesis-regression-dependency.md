# Printed model-chain dependency, 2026-09-28

The [2019 AquAdvantage salmon thesis](https://memorial.scholaris.ca/items/eef8b6b0-8b69-499a-a6a3-2bf0e1567743), page 64, prints Equation 9 for body lipid (BL) with temperature (T), lipid intake (LI) and dietary protein-to-lipid ratio (PL); Equation 10 separately estimates LI from body weight (BW) and PL. Literal substitution of the displayed point estimates produces `BL (%) = 12.10558 + 0.203*T + 0.00224*BW - 0.04332*PL`. Thus BW, which the first printed fit explicitly excludes as a **direct** predictor, re-enters via estimated LI if the two equations are chained. That is an algebraic dependency, not a reanalysis of fish-level data or an inconsistency in the original fit. The published R² values 0.940 and 0.986 belong to separate models and cannot be multiplied to assert a chained prediction accuracy. Without external data and uncertainty propagation, the composed equation should not be treated as a validated prediction or used as rearing advice. No project gate credit.
