# Thesis Table 2-7 fillet-yield units, 2026-09-28

The [archived 2019 thesis](https://memorial.scholaris.ca/items/eef8b6b0-8b69-499a-a6a3-2bf0e1567743), printed page 63, says fillet yields at 10.5 and 13.5°C were higher than at 16.5°C by "7% and 4%, respectively." Table 2-7 prints means of 59.27%, 56.59%, and 52.11% at those temperatures. Subtracting the 16.5°C mean gives 7.16 and 4.48 percentage **points**, broadly matching the prose's integers. Relative percent increases over the 16.5°C mean are instead 13.74% and 8.60%. The prose's exact rounding convention is not specified, so this is a unit-interpretation note, not an allegation of error. The script also transcribes the means, SEM, sample sizes, and printed significance letters for yield, astaxanthin, and colour score. It does not recreate statistics from fish-level data or recommend a rearing temperature. Zero gate credit.
