# AquAdvantage salmon RNA-seq: tank and fish are different units

The [2022 original article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9168996/) describes three temperature conditions (10.5, 13.5 and 16.5 °C), each in **three tanks**. The authors collected 21 liver samples per condition, then selected **two fish per tank**, six per condition, for 18 RNA-seq libraries. Selection restricted fish to within one standard deviation of post-sampling mean weight; if more than two qualified in a tank, two were randomly selected. Table 1 prints 18 distinct SRR run accessions. The source does not print a Table 1 run-to-tank mapping, so no tank-aware model can be replayed from the published table alone.

The size-matched design also makes the group comparison a joint temperature/time-to-size contrast: 800 g fish were sampled at approximately **10, 11 and 12 months** in warm, middle and cool treatments, respectively. We cannot disentangle temperature from age or exposure duration by labeling the 18 fish independent temperature assignments. This is an inferential boundary, not a refutation of the authors' observed differential-expression results. The archived source paragraphs, run labels, selection fraction 6/21 and audit are replayed by `scripts/audit_salmon_tank_sample_units.py` and a test. No sequencing payloads were fetched, no tank model or new biological result was produced, and all project gate credit remains zero.
