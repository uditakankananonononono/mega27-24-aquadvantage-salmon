# Two published salmon near-comparators, 2026-09-28

The [2013 juvenile GH-transgenic triploid salmon study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24145116/) compares liver expression across fast-growing and slow-growing families using a 32K cDNA microarray, then selected qPCR. It offers a distinct published family/growth context, but neither the temperature-gradient task nor the RNA-seq assay in our [2022 target study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9168996/). Its abstract does not give a sample-level expression matrix.

The [2019 AquAdvantage antiviral study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31891812/) does use the same 10.5/13.5/16.5°C treatments, but the target paper itself says its fish were a subset at 800 g from the **same overarching experiment**. The antiviral work challenged fish with pIC or PBS and assayed time-dependent head-kidney qPCR rather than basal liver RNA-seq. It is relevant within-experiment biology, not a fully independent rearing-cohort replication or a directly comparable expression endpoint. The two source abstracts and the target XML are checked in a replay script (`scripts/audit_related_salmon_cohort_scope.py`). Neither candidate currently satisfies a fair held-out same-task validation, comparator win, individually fetched accession payload, new discovery or gate credit.
