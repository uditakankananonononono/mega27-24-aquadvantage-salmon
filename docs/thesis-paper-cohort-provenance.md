# Thesis, growth article and transcript article are linked sources, 2026-09-28

The [2022 AquAdvantage salmon transcriptomics paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9168996/) explicitly says its fillet samples were collected from the **same fish** sampled for liver expression, and refers readers to Ignatz et al. (2020b), DOI `10.1016/j.aquaculture.2019.734896`, for composition. It also describes an immune-response subset as drawn from the same overarching temperature experiment. The [2019 thesis](https://memorial.scholaris.ca/items/eef8b6b0-8b69-499a-a6a3-2bf0e1567743) contains the original growth/composition context. These measurements have common provenance, not an independent held-out validation cohort merely because they appear in separate publications. The thesis includes other stages and fish; this note does **not** assert that every thesis fish is one of the 18 RNA-seq libraries. This is a leakage-control and provenance clarification, not a model fit, product-level result, independent accession use, comparator or discovery. Zero gate credit.
