# Salmon temperature comparison: published supplemental-table audit, 2026-09-26

The [original 2022 paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9168996/) states that differential-expression comparisons among 10.5, 13.5 and 16.5 C groups used both edgeR and DESeq2 at q<0.05 and fold change >=1.50 or <=0.66, and only concordant DET lists entered its downstream analysis. I fetched the [Europe PMC source supplementary archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9168996/supplementaryFiles), extracted Tables S1-S3, archived them byte-for-byte and checked their hashes in `results/supplement_tables_audit.json`. The tables contain author-processed, selected transcript summaries, NOT all per-sample RNA-seq counts. The source's main text reports 1750 / 172 / 52 concordant DETs across the three comparisons; our first simple q-only audit counted 1771 / 174 / 53, demonstrating that q alone is not the paper's full criterion. The replay script now applies both q and its published fold-change threshold per method. The remaining comparison to the article's counts is a source consistency check, not an independent discovery.

The original paper also states that its 18 libraries are all female triploid growth-hormone transgenic Atlantic salmon from one cohort. The transcript tables are not 4,678/1,010/538 accession datasets, nor do processed fold changes establish product-wide growth or environmental safety. No raw sample matrix, independent cohort, strongest-comparator win or novel finding is established. Any future model must use per-sample expression or a different measurable endpoint and independently held-out source.

**Observed source discrepancy:** exact application of displayed q<0.05 plus FC>=1.50 or <=0.66 to both tools yielded 1733 / 171 / 51 rows, still short of the main-text 1750 / 172 / 52. Possible reasons include display-rounded fold changes, spreadsheet highlighting logic, or original filtering conventions; none is yet verified. Do not silently reconcile these numbers or cite our counts as reproductions of the authors' result. An openpyxl style audit or author/source clarification is needed.

**Resolution of count provenance, not of full filtering semantics (2026-09-26):** Inspecting original spreadsheet cell fill shows green-highlighted rows count exactly 1,750 / 172 / 52, matching the article's reported concordant DET totals. Author legend labels green as jointly significant, yellow as DESeq2-only, red as edgeR-only. `scripts/reconcile_author_highlights.py` hashes and independently counts these fills; a replay test checks the totals. However, 17 / 1 / 1 green rows fail a literal combined q-and-displayed-FC criterion despite both displayed q values meeting it, so we cannot reproduce the original row selection solely from rounded/numeric columns. Treat green as the authors' classification, not a new significance result; method-level semantics remain an open gap.
