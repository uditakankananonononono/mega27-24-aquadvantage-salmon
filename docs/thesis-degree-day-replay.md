# Thesis growth-table degree-day accounting replay, 2026-09-27

The [2019 Memorial University thesis](https://memorial.scholaris.ca/items/eef8b6b0-8b69-499a-a6a3-2bf0e1567743) (archived PDF, SHA-256 pinned in `results/thesis_degree_day_replay.json`) prints cumulative degree-days by month post-first feeding for three rearing temperatures (Table 2-1) and days to a mean 1500 g (results text: 475, 418, 382 days at 10.5, 13.5, 16.5 C). Its methods state fry were held at 13 C, introduced to feed over 14 days, then ramped over 24 h to setpoints held within +-0.5 C. This audit replays only the printed arithmetic.

**Month 1.** The printed month-1 cumulative degree-days (514, 614, 718 C.d) match the methods timeline - 14 days at 13 C, one ramp day, then about 31 days at setpoint - with residuals of -5.25, +0.25 and +9.75 C.d, all inside the +-0.5 C tolerance over a ~46-day span. Implied month-1 spans are 45.5, 46.0 and 46.6 days across treatments, mutually consistent to about a day.

**Terminal values.** Final cumulative degree-days (5175, 5838, 6565 C.d) match printed days-to-1500 g x setpoint + 182 C.d (the 14-day 13 C introduction) + one ramp day with residuals of 6.25, 0.25 and 65.25 C.d, all within the +-0.5 C tolerance over 475/418/382 days. This is consistent with days-to-target being counted from temperature establishment after the acclimation period; the document does not print that anchor explicitly, so it is an inference from the close numeric match, not a stated fact.

**Month cadence.** Non-terminal monthly increments average 30.6, 30.6 and 29.7 day-equivalents per treatment, but range from 21.6 to 37.2 day-equivalents; 8/14, 7/12 and 9/11 non-terminal months fall outside the constant-setpoint +-0.5 C calendar-month band. The printed "months" behave like sampling intervals rather than calendar months at constant temperature, and daily temperature logs are not printed, so per-month calendar consistency cannot be resolved from the document. This is a descriptive observation, not an accusation of error. The dash pattern (tables stop after months 16, 14, 13) is consistent with the printed days-to-target (15.6, 13.7, 12.6 calendar months).

**Negative result.** The printed TGC values themselves are NOT replayable from the document: Table 2-1 prints degree-days and TGC but not the monthly mean weights the TGC formula needs, and weights appear only as Figure 2-1 graphics. The thesis therefore cannot be internally audited for TGC correctness from printed numbers alone; underlying logs or per-fish data would be required.

The script transcribes the printed table, replays the arithmetic, pins the PDF hash, and is covered by a hermetic test. This is source-integrity accounting on published numbers: no growth, line, or product-level inference, no independent dataset, no comparator win, no gate credit.
